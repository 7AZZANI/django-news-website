Standards & Accountability

Editorial Code of Ethics

Integrity, source protection, factual rigor, and prompt transparent correction.

1. Accuracy and Verification

We adhere to strict multi-source verification standards. Factual assertions in news reports require direct confirmation from primary documentary evidence, verifiable datasets, or on-the-record testimony from qualified sources.

2. Anonymous Sources

Anonymity is granted only when sources face plausible danger, career retaliation, or legal jeopardy, and when the information they provide is vital to the public interest and cannot be obtained by other means.

3. Corrections Policy

When errors of fact occur, The Chronicle corrects them promptly and transparently. Corrections are documented at the foot of the revised article, stating the nature of the error and the date of correction.

4. Separation of News and Opinion

The Chronicle maintains an absolute divide between factual news reporting and commentary or analysis. Opinion essays and analytical commentary are explicitly labeled to avoid reader confusion.

5. Conflict of Interest

Correspondents and editors are prohibited from holding financial investments in companies or sectors they actively cover, and may not accept gifts, compensated travel, or honoraria from subjects of coverage.
